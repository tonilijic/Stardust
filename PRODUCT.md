# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audience tracks the homepage already routes for and the redesign preserves: (1) developers
evaluating a full-stack TypeScript framework, and (2) AI coding agents / "vibe coders" who need
the spec layer to be legible to a model, not just a human. Same product, addressed twice.

## Product Purpose

Wasp compiles a high-level `*.wasp.ts` specification into a working React + Node.js + Prisma app
with auth, jobs, email, and deployment wired in. The redesign's job is to make that pitch land
faster and more credibly on the homepage — same product truth as `stardust/current/PRODUCT.md`,
carried forward unchanged.

## Positioning

Unchanged from current state: a typed, full-stack specification layer that compiles to ordinary,
readable code — not a black-box runtime, not a hosted platform lock-in.

## Operating Context

Unchanged: local CLI-driven development, `*.wasp.ts` spec files alongside standard React/Node/
Prisma source, one-command deploy to any host.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Unchanged from `stardust/current/PRODUCT.md`. This redesign is homepage-surface only — it does
not touch product capability claims.

## Brand Commitments

**Binding, from the team's own design-system post** (`https://wasp.sh/blog/2026/07/13/why-design-matters-for-a-web-framework`):
Wasp Yellow (`#f5c842`), Ink Black (`#111`), Blueprint Paper (`#f7f5f0`) are the entire declared
color system — "three colors, and that's it." JetBrains Mono (backed by IBM Plex Mono) is the
declared heading face. "90° angles, 2px strokes, no rounded-blob softness anywhere" is a stated
design law, not a style preference. Light theme-first is deliberate, not a gap. All three
redesign variants are Mode A (brand-faithful): these commitments are pinned, not explored.

## Evidence on Hand

Real testimonials, a YC badge, and linked example/production apps — all captured in
`stardust/current/pages/index.json` and reusable at the same semantic position per the Mode A
image-reuse contract. No fabricated content will be introduced.

## Product Principles

- Ship the brand's own stated law, not its Docusaurus-theme approximation — every token traces
  back to the declared 3-color / sharp-corner system, not a generated derivative.
- Keep both audience tracks (developer, AI agent) visible and distinct — neither absorbs the
  other.
- Let the "specification document" concept govern structure, not just decoration — the numbered,
  spec-like framing should shape layout, not just supply a badge motif.
- Preserve every real testimonial, example link, and production reference at its current
  semantic position — the site's credibility signals are not decorative.
